# PassVault privacy policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

PassVault is an offline password manager for Android and iOS. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data. It has no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

## Data you store

Everything you save in PassVault (passwords, cards, notes, two-factor keys) stays on your device, encrypted. It is never sent anywhere. The developer has no access to it and cannot recover it if you forget your master password. PassVault's data is excluded from the device's cloud backup.

## Network

- **Google Play version:** has no internet permission and cannot connect to any network.
- **Version downloaded from GitHub:** connects only to GitHub, to check for a newer version of the app and download it when you choose to update. The request contains no vault data; like any web request, it shows GitHub your IP address.

## Camera and files

- The camera is used only while you scan a QR code (two-factor setup or phone-to-phone transfer). Images are processed on the device and are not saved or sent.
- Files (imports, screenshots of QR codes, backups) are read only when you choose them, and exports are written only where you choose to save or share them.

## Biometrics

Fingerprint and face unlock are handled by the operating system. PassVault never receives your biometric data.

## Clipboard

Passwords you copy are marked as sensitive and removed from the clipboard after the delay you choose.

## Autofill

If you turn on autofill on Android, PassVault reads the login form of the app or site you are signing in to, on your device only, to find its username and password fields and to suggest matching logins. It fills only the login you pick, after you unlock, and keeps nothing from the other app.

## Children

PassVault is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published in this file, with a new date above. Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/daocha/password-vault/issues.
